Connect your Instagram account

Works with Business and Creator accounts. Studio reads your insights through Meta's official API. It only reads, never posts, and your token goes straight from this browser to Meta.

How do I get a token?
  1. Open developers.facebook.com, create an app (type: Business) and add the Instagram product.
  2. Under API setup with Instagram login, add your Instagram professional account and click Generate token.
  3. Allow instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_manage_insights, then paste the token here.

Tokens expire (about 60 days for long-lived ones). Anyone with the token can read your insights, so don't save it on a shared computer.
